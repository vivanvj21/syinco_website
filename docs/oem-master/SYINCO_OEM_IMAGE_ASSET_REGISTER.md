## 2. Image Asset Quality & Strategy Assessment

1. **Current Assets Are Low-Resolution**: The WordPress upload path (`wp-content/uploads/catch-*-300x300*.png`) confirms that these images are small thumbnail previews. Using them directly on high-density 1440px B2B instrumentation pages would degrade visual authority.
2. **Missing Architectural Diagrams**: For complex scientific instruments (such as ZEM-3, TCN-2ω, and SuperLIX), physical principle schematics (e.g. optical path, 4-terminal probe geometry) are critical for engineering evaluation.
3. **Interim Placeholder Strategy**: Until Advance Riko supplies 1200px+ photography, SYINCO should render clean technical placeholders featuring schematic vector outlines and model identifiers.
