## 3. Technology Verification Summary

### A. Thermoelectric & Energy Characterization
- `AR-ZEM-3`: **VERIFIED**. The benchmark Seebeck coefficient / electrical resistance measurement system. Global academic validation confirmed.
- `AR-ZEM-D`: **VERIFIED**. Specialised fixture for polymeric thin thermoelectric sheets.
- `AR-F-PEM`, `AR-PEM-2`, `AR-MINI-PEM`: **VERIFIED**. Power generation efficiency systems for modules.

### B. Thermal Properties & Diffusivity
- `AR-TC-1200RH-TC-9000`: **VERIFIED**. Laser flash thermal constant measurement system.
- `AR-TD-1`: **VERIFIED**. Xenon flash method for anisotropic thermal diffusivity.
- `AR-TCN-2OMEGA`: **VERIFIED**. World's only commercial cross-plane 2-omega nanoscale thin-film thermal conductivity analyzer.
- `AR-LASERPIT`: **VERIFIED**. AC method in-plane thermal diffusivity for sheets and films.
- `AR-FTC`: **VERIFIED**. AC Joule periodic heating method for polymers, paper, and thin films.
- `AR-GH-1`: **VERIFIED**. Guarded heat flow meter complying with ASTM E1530.
- `AR-F-CAL`: **VERIFIED**. Heat flow rate system for multilayer substrates.

### C. Thermal Expansion & Dilatometry
- `AR-SUPERLIX`: **VERIFIED**. Dual-beam laser interferometer dilatometer with sub-nanometer resolution (10⁻⁸/K).
- `AR-LIX`: **VERIFIED**. Laser interferometer dilatometer complying explicitly with JIS R3251-1995.
- `AR-DL-9000-DLY-9000`: **VERIFIED**. Push-rod thermodilatometer series.
- `AR-DLY-9000-ROBOT`: **VERIFIED**. Automated carousel-loaded dilatometer for unattended industrial QC.
