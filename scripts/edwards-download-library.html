    Our Safety Manual contains safety information required for the safe and correct specification, design, operation and maintenance of vacuum pumps and vacuum systems. 

    In this document, we identify potential hazards and provide guidelines to minimise safety hazards and how to suitably deal with them should they occur.

    This document is intended to be read by anyone who specifies, designs, installs, operates, or maintains vacuum pumps and systems. we recommend that it is read in conjunction with: 

    • the instruction manuals supplied with your equipment
    • information provided by the suppliers of your process gases and chemicals
    • information supplied by your safety department

    If you require any further information on the suitability of our products for your process application, or any safety aspects of our vacuum pumps and systems, the please contact us.
